03 / A FEW THINGS WE CARE ABOUT

Small software.
Considered choices.

01

Keep it close.

Speech recognition runs on your device. Model downloads use the network; pairing sends approved personalization directly between devices on a reachable local network.

02

Give models a rest.

Economy loads models when needed and releases them after use. Prefer quicker repeat sessions? Choose a short retention window. Actual speed depends on your hardware and model.

03

Make room for your words.

Add vocabulary, snippets, links and replacements. Personalization is encrypted locally, and transfers need receiver approval. No account is required.

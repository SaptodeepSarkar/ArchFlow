LOCAL BY DESIGN · HONEST ABOUT PERFORMANCE

Runs on your
computer, not a cloud.

Vaani processes audio locally with your chosen recognition model. That keeps dictation private and makes performance a property of your own setup—not a remote server.

Speed

Recognition time varies with your CPU or GPU, model size, audio length, and background workload. Smaller models typically respond faster; larger ones may improve accuracy.

Baseline

Arch Linux, Hyprland/Wayland, PipeWire and a modern 64-bit CPU. For comfortable everyday use alongside other apps, 16 GB RAM is recommended.

Optional GPU

Vaani works on CPU. NVIDIA CUDA acceleration is optional; a laptop GPU with about 6 GB VRAM is a practical target for the supported local models.

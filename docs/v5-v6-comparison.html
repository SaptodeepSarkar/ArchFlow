Training progress

V6 moved from a synthetic 10k bootstrap to grouped reviewed-data training. The corrected trainer selects the best checkpoint by development exact-plan rate, then token accuracy. The guarded 20-epoch run completed without an OOM and produced the dev-selected 144/835 grouped-test result.

Training completed and evaluated

Honest conclusion

V6’s learned planner is not yet promotion-ready by itself: 0/18 on the independent challenge. The complete V6 hybrid is exact on that challenge because narrow deterministic cues handle known punctuation, lists, fillers, and emoji cases.

Do not report 18/18 as learned accuracy

Measurement note

The V5 and V6 scores come from different historical suites and should not be treated as a single apples-to-apples leaderboard. V5 figures are the recorded Qwen aggregate/intent results; V6 figures are the grouped reviewed test and independent source-grounded challenge. The authoritative details are in V6_BASELINE.md and v5-benchmark.md.

Vaani brand kit: warm paper surfaces, plum intent, lilac actions, apricot emphasis. The visual system supports trustworthy dictation; it does not replace the evaluation gates.

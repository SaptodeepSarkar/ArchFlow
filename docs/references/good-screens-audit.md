# Good-screens reference audit

Inspected on 2026-09-21. The set contains 24 portrait PNGs: `screen1color.png`
through `screen9.png` are 1440 × 3120; `screen10.png` through `screen23.png`
are 1080 × 2400. They are reference material only. The product name, logo,
purple progress treatment, lavender CTA fill, distinctive decorative cards,
illustrations, and layouts remain specific to Wispr Flow and must not be reused.

## Overall visual language

The eye is consistently led by a sparse title area, one explanatory unit, and a
full-width bottom action. Light screens use a warm off-white field, fine dark
outlines, large white intervals, and editorial serif headlines. Dark welcome
screens use a dim editorial photo with white type and a pale CTA. Motion is
suggested by step progress, page changes, and press targets—not ambient
animation. Vaani should retain that restraint while using its own sans type,
cobalt/coral palette, geometric mark, and original compositions.

## Transferable observations

- A single decisive bottom action is more effective than a field of competing cards.
- Empty space is functional: it separates the promise from setup instructions and makes state changes obvious.
- Thin outlines and mostly neutral surfaces make a small accent feel consequential.
- Setup images should explain one real system transition at a time; decorative art must never replace permission truth.

Vaani visual language

Writing should feel lighter after you speak.

Warm paper surfaces, a confident plum action colour, and clearly bounded controls make Vaani feel composed without feeling distant.

Ribbon V / app mark
01 / Identity

A soft V, held by structure.

The revised mark keeps the Vaani initial, but expresses it with rounded ribbon strokes and an apricot end point. It stays clear at 24 px and never imitates an audio waveform.

02 / Colour

Paper, plum, lilac, apricot.

These tones borrow the references’ warmth and clarity while using Vaani’s own names and values. Plum is for progress and intent; lilac is the action fill; apricot introduces a feature or a human moment.

Colour discipline: only one lively surface per screen. Lilac actions use a dark outline; plum is never used as a large app background outside intentional moments.
03 / Type

Editorial warmth, product clarity.

A restrained serif is reserved for onboarding and feature moments. Manrope remains the operational typeface for settings, navigation, and the voice overlay.

04 / Actions

Broad, bounded, unmistakable.

Full-width buttons are reserved for the decision that advances the flow. Their plum outline and lilac fill are tactile and recognisable, but the Vaani corner radius and typography remain distinct.

One decision per screen.

Utility screens can have quiet actions. Onboarding and permission screens have one primary action anchored to the bottom.

  • Primary: lilac fill + 2 px ink outline.
  • Secondary: paper fill + outline.
  • Destructive and unsafe states never use lilac.
05 / Selection

Options are calm, not card-heavy.

Use tall, outlined rows for simple choices. Their selected state is a quiet lilac wash, not a dashboard card treatment.

06 / Voice box

The voice box is dark, brief, and factual.

It borrows the reference screens’ strong contrast and soft corner language, while retaining Vaani’s compact non-focus-stealing overlay contract.

Active dictation

One compact state surface.

Real microphone movement belongs inside the box. Preview text remains optional, and error states explain exactly what happened.

Do not turn this into a chat composer, permanent bubble, or brand illustration.
